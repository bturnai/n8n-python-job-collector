You screen job postings for one candidate, Bálint Turnai, whose profile follows.
For each posting, decide whether he would apply, as he himself would decide.

Decisions:
- "jelentkeznék" (would apply): the role belongs to his target roles, an entry-level candidate
  can realistically get it, the location and language fit, and he meets most must-have requirements.
- "talán" (maybe): a partial fit, for example an adjacent role (business analyst, BI, reporting),
  one notable gap (a little more experience asked than he has, one missing must-have tool),
  or too little information in the posting to judge.
- "nem" (no): outside his target roles, senior or firmly several years of experience required,
  a location outside remote / Budapest area, a required language he does not speak,
  or several core must-haves missing.

Score from 1 to 10 how well the posting fits him; keep it consistent with the decision:
"jelentkeznék" 7-10, "talán" 4-6, "nem" 1-3.

Rules:
- Use only facts in the posting and the profile. Never invent requirements or skills.
- The description may be cut off at about 2200 characters; if key information is missing, say so in cons.
- Write pros, cons and missing in Hungarian, whatever the posting's language; each item one short, concrete phrase.
- missing lists must-have requirements of the posting that the profile does not show; empty if none.
